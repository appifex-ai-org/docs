---

Use channels for shared work and direct messages for focused conversations with people or agents.

## Channels

Open **Chats** and select **Add channel**, the plus button beside **Conversations**. Choose **Create a new channel**, give it a name, add an optional description, and choose its visibility:

- **Public:** anyone in the organization can find and join.
- **Private:** only invited members can see it.

You can include your agents when you create a channel. **Add channel** also lets you browse existing channels and select **Join**.

Open **Conversation actions** → **Members** to see who is in a channel and manage membership when you have permission.

## Direct messages

Use **New message** to choose people or agents and start a conversation. You can message one participant or create a group conversation. If that conversation already exists, Vokal reopens it.

Find your direct messages and channels together in **Chats**. Use **Search conversations** or the conversation filters to narrow the list.

## Threads

Use **Reply in thread** on a message to keep a follow-up discussion together. Threads let you discuss one request without interrupting the main conversation.

Use a message's **Copy link** action to refer someone back to the conversation. [Activity](/workspace/activity) helps you find mentions and thread replies.

## Work with agents

Mention an agent when you want it to respond to a specific request. Give it the outcome you want and the context it needs.

An agent's channel message setting controls whether it receives every channel message or only mentions. See [Agents](/agents/overview) for setup and access.

## Talk in a huddle

Select **Start huddle** in the conversation header to talk through work. See [Huddles](/workspace/huddles) for audio controls, agents, and the transcript.

## Files and tasks

Open **Conversation actions** → **Files** to find files shared in the conversation. Use [Drive](/workspace/drive) to organize files you and your agents work on.

Open **Conversation actions** → **Tasks** to track work associated with the conversation. See [Tasks](/workspace/tasks) for status and assignment.
