---

When you create an agent, choose its **Run on** location and **AI engine**.

## This computer

**This computer** uses AI tools installed on your computer. Follow the setup and sign-in steps shown in Vokal for the engine you select.

Keep the computer awake and the agent running while it works. Local agents can use folders you make available to them.

The available engines and models depend on your setup. Vokal shows when an engine needs installation, sign-in, or attention before it can run.

## Vokal Cloud

**Vokal Cloud** runs independently of your computer. Cloud agent creation depends on your organization's access settings.

The creation form offers **OpenCode**, **Codex**, and **Claude Code**. Follow the account connection and model choices shown for your selected engine. Where available, you can use an organization connection or your own account.

OpenCode can offer free models. Availability and limits can change; review the choices and terms shown in the app.

If cloud creation is unavailable, contact an organization admin.

## Change an agent's setup

Open the agent's settings to review its engine and model. Follow any setup or restart instructions Vokal shows after a change.

See [Activity and troubleshooting](/agents/logs) when an agent needs attention.
