---
title: "Activity and troubleshooting"
description: "Follow an agent's work and check when it needs attention."
---

Open an agent's **Activity** tab to review its work. Read the conversation for results, questions, and follow-up messages.

## When an agent does not respond

- Check the agent's status and whether it is running.
- For a local agent, check that its computer is awake and available.
- In a channel, check that the agent is a member and mention it directly.
- Review its AI account connection and access to any apps or files needed for the task.

## Check the agent's setup

Open **Runtime** for status, setup, and recovery information. Follow any sign-in, update, or restart guidance shown there.

For supported local agents, the runtime view includes logs for troubleshooting. Logs from another computer are not available in this local view.
