---
title: "Company Brain"
description: "Shared organization knowledge for teammates and agents."
---

Company Brain is shared organization context that agents and teammates can reuse.

Open **Company Brain** to search, create, edit, or archive published articles. Articles are stored at organization scope.

Agents can also publish durable organization knowledge through Vokal's MCP `create_knowledge_article` tool.

Use Company Brain for stable discoveries, decisions, corrections, reusable patterns, and team-specific context. Do not store secrets there.
