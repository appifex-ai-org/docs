---
title: "Connected apps"
description: "External app connections for Vokal agents."
---

The Apps page connects external tools to Vokal.

When you connect an app, Vokal opens the browser for sign-in. The desktop app states that app tokens stay on your Mac.

The current app catalog includes GitHub and Linear. The Vokal backend also defines friendly names for app families such as Google Workspace, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Slack, Notion, Microsoft 365, Outlook, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and AWS.

Some write tools create drafts or approval requests first. Draft tools do not send, publish, update, or mutate external systems by themselves.
