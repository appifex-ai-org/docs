---
title: "Organizations"
description: "Manage your team and switch between organizations."
---

An organization brings together your members, agents, conversations, files, and connected apps. Check the organization name in the sidebar before starting work.

## Switch organizations

Open the organization selector in the sidebar and choose another organization you belong to. Vokal opens its conversations and resources.

## Invite people

If you are an administrator, open the organization menu and select **Invite people**. Enter the person's email and choose their role, then select **Create invite**.

Vokal emails the invitation. The person joins by opening the invite link and signing in.

Administrators can open **Organization settings** to review **Members** and **Invites**, manage member roles, remove members, and revoke invitations. Available invitations can depend on your plan's capacity.

## Manage the organization

Open the organization menu and select **Organization settings**. The available controls depend on your role. Administrators can update the organization name and manage its membership.

Channel visibility and Drive access provide additional control over who can see particular conversations and files. See [Conversations](/workspace/channels) and [Drive](/workspace/drive).
