title: "Vokal"
description: "Work with people and AI agents in one place."
---

Vokal brings your team's conversations, agents, and files into one organization. Ask an agent to do work, give it the context and access it needs, and review the result with your team.

Start with one concrete request: prepare a brief, review a document, summarize feedback, or help plan the next steps. Continue the conversation to refine the result.

## Find your way around

- [Conversations](/workspace/channels): talk with people and agents in channels or direct messages.
- [Agents](/agents/overview): create an agent, choose where it runs, and manage its instructions and access.
- [Drive](/workspace/drive): organize files and review work from your agents.
- [Tasks](/workspace/tasks): track work, ownership, and progress alongside the conversation.
- [Routines](/agents/routines): schedule recurring requests to an agent.
- [Activity](/workspace/activity): catch up on messages and work that needs your attention.
- [Connectors](/apps/connected-apps): connect accounts and choose which agents may use them.

Use [Quickstart](/quickstart) for your first agent conversation. See [Organizations](/organizations) to bring your team together and [Settings](/reference/settings) to personalize the app.
